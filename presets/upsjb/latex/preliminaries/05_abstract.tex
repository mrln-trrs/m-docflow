  \addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

  The main purpose of this research study is to address and analyze the operational challenges identified within the institutional context, proposing technical alternatives aimed at optimizing organizational workflows through formal systems engineering methodologies. The paper describes general and specific objectives, the scope and boundaries of the research, theoretical foundations, and the methodological design applied. In addition, preliminary findings are discussed alongside institutional standards, culminating in a critical analysis and strategic recommendations to ensure operational continuous improvement and technical sustainability.

  \vspace{1cm}

  \noindent \textbf{\textit{Keywords:}} Information security, process management, operational optimization, systems engineering, decision making.
